## Brand & Style

This design system channels an unmistakably pure, utility-driven aesthetic inspired by classic Google workspace interfaces and material minimalism. Tailored for a modern SEO audit SaaS platform presented in Slovak ("SEO audit, ktorý prináša výsledky"), the tone is direct, analytical, and effortlessly accessible. It strips away ornamental noise to let data, audit scores, and clear calls to action take precedence.

The visual direction merges high-whitespace modern minimalism with Google's distinct primary accent cadence. Interfaces rely on pure white backdrops, ultra-subtle structural lines, and generous optical breathing room. It cultivates total technical confidence, clarity, and speed for digital marketers, webmasters, and enterprise SEO leads.

## Colors

The color system establishes a pristine light baseline, pairing Google Blue (`#1A73E8`) as the authoritative primary action hue with the iconic secondary chromatic trio: Green (`#34A853`), Yellow (`#FBBC04`), and Red (`#EA4335`). These vibrant tones are reserved for analytical status indicators, data visualizations, and contextual feedback:
- **Google Blue (`#1A73E8`)**: Direct interactions, primary links, key conversion actions, and active navigation states.
- **Google Green (`#34A853`)**: Passed checks, healthy Core Web Vitals, indexation success, and positive delta values.
- **Google Yellow (`#FBBC04`)**: Warnings, warnings on redirect chains, and impending SEO risks.
- **Google Red (`#EA4335`)**: Critical crawl errors, 4xx/5xx responses, and missing vital metadata.

Neutral text uses `#202124` for crisp contrast against pure `#FFFFFF` canvas areas, with `#5F6368` serving for secondary copy, supporting metrics, and labels. Structural lines exclusively use `#F1F3F4` and `#E5E7EB` to maintain structure without imposing visual weight.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes `Roboto Flex` across all viewports to achieve absolute geometric clarity, neutral precision, and complete diacritical support for Slovak (`á`, `č`, `ď`, `é`, `í`, `ĺ`, `ľ`, `ň`, `ó`, `ô`, `ŕ`, `š`, `ť`, `ú`, `ý`, `ž`). 

Headlines maintain a clean medium weight (`500` to `600`) with tight negative tracking to deliver impactful hero copy. Body typography defaults to `15px` (`body-md`) with comfortable leading for audit documentation, metric explanations, and diagnostic logs. Micro-labels and table metadata use deliberate uppercase tracking to preserve legibility at small sizes.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1240px`), collapsing into 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports. Spacing emphasizes vast white space and an airy hierarchy:

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: Max content container of `1240px`, centered with `2rem` outer margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Section paddings range comfortably between `4rem` (`space-2xl`) and `6rem` (`space-3xl`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `1.5rem` margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Interactive elements adapt to full-width stacking.

Vertical rhythm adheres to a strict 4px/8px modular base scale, ensuring precise baseline alignment across audit score summaries and comparison grids.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows an understated, ambient model that avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp separation and gentle elevation shifts:

- **Level 0 (Flat)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` canvas with hairline dividers using `#F1F3F4` or `#E5E7EB`.
- **Level 1 (Search Pill & Default Cards)**: Soft ambient elevation (`0 1px 3px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.08), 0 1px 2px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.05)`). Used for the central audit input field and resting cards.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Focus States)**: Expanded ambient lift (`0 4px 12px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.12), 0 1px 3px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.08)`).
- **Level 3 (Modals & Audit Flyouts)**: Floating dialogs and popovers (`0 8px 24px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.16)`).

Border treatments are hairline (1px solid) and transition subtly from `#F1F3F4` to `#E5E7EB` or `#1A73E8` upon user engagement.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a dual shape language: prominent pill-shaped geometry (`roundedness: 3`, full `9999px` radii) for primary call-to-action buttons, search audit inputs, and metric chips; complemented by soft `8px` (`rounded-lg`) to `12px` (`rounded-xl`) radii on data cards and modal dialogs. This hybrid approach mirrors the familiar search bar silhouette while maintaining structured discipline for tabular audit figures.

## Components

### Audit Search Input (Hero Component)
- **Geometry**: Pill-shaped container (`border-radius: 9999px`), 56px height on desktop, 48px on mobile.
- **Styling**: Pure `#FFFFFF` fill, 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, elevated with Level 1 shadow (`0 1px 6px rgba(32, 33, 36, 0.12)`).
- **Interactive State**: On focus, transitions border to `1px solid #1A73E8` with a deepened ambient shadow (`0 2px 10px rgba(60, 64, 67, 0.2)`).
- **Contents**: Search icon (`#5F6368`) on the left, clear placeholder (`napr. vasadomena.sk`), and an embedded pill CTA button on the right.

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: `#1A73E8` background, `#FFFFFF` text, pill radius (`9999px`), padding `10px 24px`, font weight `500`. Hover state lightens to `#1765CC` with slight ambient shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Pure transparent background, 1px solid `#DADCE0`, `#1A73E8` text, pill radius. Hover shifts background to `#F8F9FA`.
- **Destructive**: `#EA4335` background with white text, or outline variant for lower prominence.

### Cards & Audit Blocks
- **Resting**: Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#F1F3F4`, border-radius `12px`, padding `24px`.
- **Hover**: 1px solid `#E5E7EB`, transitions Level 1 shadow subtly. No dramatic scale transformations.

### Metric Chips & Status Badges
- **Shape**: Compact pill (`border-radius: 9999px`), height `24px`, horizontal padding `10px`.
- **Variants**:
  - *Healthy / Úspech*: Tinted background (`#E6F4EA`), text `#137333`.
  - *Warning / Upozornenie*: Tinted background (`#FEF7E0`), text `#B06000`.
  - *Critical / Chyba*: Tinted background (`#FCE8E6`), text `#C5221F`.
  - *Informational / Info*: Tinted background (`#E8F0FE`), text `#1A73E8`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes**: 18px dimensions, 2px border `#5F6368` when unchecked, transitioning to solid `#1A73E8` fill with crisp white check/inner dot when active. Smooth 150ms ease transition.

### SEO Score Ring / Gauge Component
- Circular SVG progress indicators using `#34A853`, `#FBBC04`, and `#EA4335` based on 0-100 score brackets, accompanied by bold numerical display and clean subtitle labels.
